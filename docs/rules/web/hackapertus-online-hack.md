# Online *Hackathon*

1–16 October 2026

[Sign up](https://hackapertus.devpost.com/)

## Two weeks. One open mission.

Build with Apertus 1.5, Switzerland's fully open language model. Test its readiness, adapt it to Swiss contexts, or turn an original idea into a working open prototype.

Format
:   Online · 1–5 people

Models
:   Apertus 8B + 70B

Participation
:   Free and open to all

Track 1

## Apertus *Readiness*

ARed-Teaming Apertus

BSwiss Voices — Localising Apertus to Swiss Languages, Knowledge and Values

Track 2

## Apertus *Adoption*

AAcademia Challenges

BOwn Project

## Key dates

01 OCT · 12:00 CEST

### Tracks and challenges released

Choose a track, meet your team and start building remotely.

01–16 OCT

### Build with Apertus

Work with the 8B or 70B model, join online sessions and book time with mentors.

16 OCT · 12:00 CEST

### Final submission deadline

Submit your technical report, dataset where relevant, and code repository. No extensions.

BY 23 OCT

### Jury decision

The expert jury reviews all eligible submissions and selects the winners.

[Sign up](https://hackapertus.devpost.com/)
