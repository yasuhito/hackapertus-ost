    04 · Selection model

    Voting & Timeline

    01

    Cluster by challenge

    We distribute submissions so that each challenge is reviewed by 2–3 judges, depending on the amount of valid submissions. Each jury member receives a defined number of submissions to review.

    02

    Consolidate decisions

    Scores support the final winner decision for each track. In case of a tie, we discuss the winners in the final alignment call.

    Track 2A: Each academic partner is asked to judge its own challenge and provides 1 winner. The overall jury then selects the Track 2A winner from those 5 preselected projects.

    05 · Rubrics

    Scoring by track.

    TRACK 1A

    Up to 250 points

    Red-Teaming

    • ·Max 5 issues can be submitted, each scored on its own.
    • ·Categories 1–4: score = points.
    • ·An issue needs at least 1 point in each of 1–4 to score at all.
    • ·Categories 5–6: judged once, weighted N/2 (N = issues).

    Total = Part 1 + Part 2 · max 250 points.

    TRACK 1B

    Up to 100 points

    Swiss Voices

    • ·Points gained = the scoring level (1 scores 1 point).
    • ·At least Level 1 in each of categories 1–4 to score at all.
    • ·Categories 2–4 are weighted.
    • ·Total = weighted sum across all categories.

    Highest score wins · max 100 points.

    TRACK 2A

    Partner review

    Academia Challenges

    Academic partners select their winner per challenge. Based on the 5 winners, the overall jury selects the final Track 2A winner.

    06 · Voting setup

    How to vote.

    The pre-filtering process for submitted projects consists of 3 layers:

    1st layer

    Structure & licenses

    We verify that the repository follows the required structure and that the appropriate licenses are in place (for Hugging Face as well as GitHub).

    2nd layer

    Security scan

    We perform a security scan to ensure there are no malicious scripts or forgotten secrets inside the repository.

    3rd layer

    Spin-up test

    We attempt to spin up the project to confirm that it runs properly and can be evaluated by the jury.
