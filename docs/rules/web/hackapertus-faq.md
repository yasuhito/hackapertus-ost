# Your questions.

Everything you need to know about Hack Apertus — the format, the stages, and how to join.

## Answers, in short.

### What is Hack Apertus?

An open-source hackathon series to accelerate the adoption of Apertus, Switzerland's open large language model. Teams build real use cases and open blueprints for sovereign, compliant deployment across the public and private sector.

### What are the stages and dates?

* Online Hackathon: 1–16 October 2026, remote.
* On-site Hackathons: Q4/2026 – Q2/2027, in 6+ Swiss cities (Bern, Zurich, Basel, Lausanne, St.Gallen).
* Grand Finals: 14 May 2027, St.Gallen at University of St.Gallen / Square.

### Who can take part?

Everyone. Students, professionals, linguists, designers, developers and public and private organisations. Technical and non-technical people alike; interdisciplinary teams are encouraged.

### Do I need to attend in person?

For Stage 1: No, this is fully remote. The on-site hackathons and Grand Finals are in person.

### How do I register?

For the Online Hackathon, sign up here: <https://hackapertus.devpost.com/> and for On-Site choose your hack here: <https://hackapertus.ch/forhackers>

### Do you have a Code of Conduct?

Yes. All participants, partners and organisers are expected to follow our Code of Conduct to keep Hack Apertus safe, inclusive and respectful.

<https://hackapertus.ch/code-of-conduct>

### What is Apertus?

Switzerland's fully open LLM (8B and 70B), built by EPFL, ETH Zurich and CSCS. Open weights, data and training recipes, deployable on-premise without licensing fees.

For more information on the model, check this website <https://apertvs.ai/>

### Who is behind "Hack Apertus"?

Founded and run by the aiLights Association, and co-initiated by Artifact and Swiss Engineering. Main partners are Phoeniqs, CSCS, Canton of St.Gallen, Canton of Bern and Supertext, alongside 100+ partners. Part of the Swiss AI Action Plan.

## FAQ — Online Hackathon

### What is the Online Hackathon?

It's the first stage «Hack Apertus», an open-source hackathon series to evaluate, align, and build on Apertus, Switzerland's open LLM. Builders everywhere work remotely to evaluate the model and show what it can do. Everything you ship stays open and reusable.

### When does it take place?

1–16 October 2026, fully online.

* 1 OCT, 12:00 CEST — kickoff, tracks and challenges released
* 16 OCT, 12:00 CEST — final submission deadline
* 23 OCT — Winners announcement

### What are the tracks?

Track 1 — Apertus Readiness is about improving the model.

* 1A: Red-Teaming Apertus. Find and document where Apertus underperforms or behaves unexpectedly — culture and values, stereotyping and bias, privacy, IP and copyright, factual correctness. You can submit up to 5 issues.
* 1B: Swiss Voices. Evaluate any part of the lifecycle of localising Apertus to specific Swiss contexts, namely input adaptation, domain-specific core task intelligence, and behavioral steering.

Track 2 — Apertus Adoption is about showing what the model can do.

* 2A: Academia Challenges. Pick a challenge from our partners and solve it by building a prototype on Apertus.
* 2B: Own Project. Bring your own idea and build a working Apertus prototype — any domain, any use case. The project must be new and started within the hackathon period.

### Can I work alone or do I need a team?

Both work. Teams of 1–5 are allowed. If you're looking for teammates, our [Discord](https://discord.gg/hack-apertus) is the place to find them.

### Who should participate?

Open to all — recommended for AI/ML engineers, software developers, data scientists, researchers and NLP practitioners, technical students (CS, data science, computational linguistics), and non-engineer domain experts (law, medicine, dialects, public sector).

### What do I get to work with?

Both the Apertus 8B and 70B models, with inference support backed by our partners CSCS and Phoeniqs, and experienced mentors alongside you. You can book mentor sessions and join online support sessions throughout the two weeks.

### Which models can be used?

Submissions must use the Apertus model family, version 1.5. For Track 1 this means that only evaluations of Apertus 1.5 are accepted. For Track 2 this means that submitted solutions must be built with Apertus. Other open-weights models can be used to support development, eg as automatic judges during evaluation. Their role must be clearly described in the submission report.

### Is a community text-only GGUF of Apertus v1.5 acceptable as "Apertus v1.5"?

For Track 1A and 1B, please use the official Apertus checkpoint — community or quantized builds like Colby/apertus-v1.5-8b-text-Q4\_K\_M aren't accepted as "Apertus v1.5" here, since the official model is the baseline the tracks are evaluated against. Collecting your primary evidence via the official CSCS API is fine.

Community models are allowed in Track 2, so if you want to work with the GGUF build, that's the place for it.

### Are AI coding assistants allowed?

Yes.

### How do I register?

Register on Devpost at [hackapertus.devpost.com](https://hackapertus.devpost.com/) — that's also where you'll submit your project. Then join our [Discord](https://discord.gg/hack-apertus), our main communication channel during the hackathon and the place to find teammates.

### Are there info sessions?

Yes. We're hosting online info sessions on 1 and 2 October, right after kickoff. We'll walk you through the tracks and challenges, the submission requirements, and how to get the most out of the two weeks — and you can ask the organising team anything live. Times and joining links are announced on Discord and our Luma channel. Can't make it? The sessions are recorded and shared afterwards on Discord. Check our [Getting started Guide](https://hackapertus.notion.site/getting-started-guide-onlinehack).

### What do I need to submit?

Each track has its own submission requirements — these will be published before kickoff. What's certain: the deadline is 16 October 2026, 12:00 CEST.

### How are submissions judged?

An expert jury reviews all submissions after the deadline. The judging criteria per track will be published before kickoff.

### What can I win?

CHF 10,000 in prizes. Each track category has a cash prize of CHF 2,500 for the winner, plus Hugging Face credits, certificates and dedicated LinkedIn features for the runners-up. The strongest projects qualify to the Grand Finals in St.Gallen in May '27.

### What happens after the Online Hackathon?

This is only the beginning. Over the following months, 6+ on-site hackathons across Switzerland (Q4/2026 – Q2/2027) pick up where the Online Hackathon leaves off, carrying the work forward. Online and on-site run as separate phases — join one or all of them. The winners from each hack compete for the Main «Hack Apertus» Award at the Grand Finals on 14 May 2027 at the University of St.Gallen / SQUARE.

### I have a question that isn't answered here.

Let's connect on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/hack-apertus) — the community and the organising team are there and happy to help. You can also reach us via [hello@hackapertus.ch](mailto:hello@hackapertus.ch).
