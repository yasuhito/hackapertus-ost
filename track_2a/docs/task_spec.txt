1. The Challenge

Why This Is Hard
Long + structured documents   Relevant information may live in sections, tables, legal or financial detail.

Qualifications + negation     Small wording differences can change the inference relation.

Distributed evidence          Multiple passages may be needed for one classification.

Multilinguality               German, French and Italian, including cross-lingual claim/booklet pairs.

3. Evaluation

How Solutions Are Judged
Primary metric
Macro-F1 across Entailment, Neutral and Contradiction on a held-out benchmark.
Evidence
Relevance and correctness of returned passage(s).
Efficiency
Input/context token usage and inference time.
Multilingual analysis
Performance may also be analysed across German, French and Italian.


    The goal is effective and efficient grounded NLI.
  3. Evaluation

  How Will the System Be Evaluated?
   Metric               How it is measured

   Macro-F1             Equal weight for entailment, neutral and contradiction. Reported separately for BEGINNER Task and ADVANCED Task .
   Primary metric


   Evidence             Does the returned evidence match the gold reference passage? For ADVANCED Task, the PDF page must also match.

   Context / Tokens     Sum of input_tokens. Self-reported counts are checked against the token-counting proxy; the proxy count wins.

   Speed                Mean and p95 of inference_time_ms.

   Languages            Results broken down by DE / FR / IT and by same-language vs. cross-lingual cases.



      Goal: maximize classification quality and evidence correctness while keeping context size and latency under control.


Held-out annotated benchmark → judged on NLI effectiveness and efficiency, with predictions grounded in the source.
