# Independent source review: primary-paper notes

Research/access date: **2026-10-15 JST**. Decision: bounded role diversity for source-anchored NLI, not a majority-vote replacement. Five primary papers retrieved directly from arXiv; version dates below are revision dates, not necessarily conference publication dates. No claim of an exhaustive literature search through 2026.

## Recommendation and scope

Keep the supplied first hypothesis fixed: an independent 8B reviewer checks number, negation, condition and speaker attribution using original-source quotes; the ordinary 8B child supplies its NLI recommendation; the 70B parent makes the final decision. Independence means withholding the child's label and explanation from evidence collection, **not** assuming statistically independent errors from shared weights.

The supplied PUBLIC-log diagnosis motivates this separation but was not re-audited here. The implemented candidate's supplied contract remains the baseline: `axis`, `candidate_ids` (at most one), literal `claim_quote` (80-character cap), literal `source_quote` (120-character cap), no reviewer NLI voting, at most two reviewer calls, and preserved final v5 SYSTEM. Paper results do not establish an Apertus/NLI gain.

## Primary findings and limits

1. **Dhuliawala et al., Chain-of-Verification Reduces Hallucination in Large Language Models** - first submitted 2023-09-20; inspected [v2, 2023-09-25](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.11495v2), §§3.3, 4.3, 6; Tables 1–4. Factored verification omits the original response from verification-answer contexts; each question can also be answered separately. Llama 65B closed-book MultiSpanQA F1: **0.39 baseline, 0.46 joint, 0.48 factored** (Table 2). Biography FactScore: **55.9 baseline, 60.8 joint, 63.7 factored, 71.4 factor+revise**; average facts fell from **16.6 to 12.3** for the last comparison (Table 3). Open verification questions beat yes/no questions in factored Wiki-Category precision (**0.22 vs 0.19**, Table 4). **Limit:** no retrieval/tool verification in the method; remaining hallucinations and extra computation acknowledged. This supports separating evidence production from answer exposure, not treating another model's assertion as external truth. Supplied-source checking is an adaptation, not their tested setting.

2. **Huang et al., Large Language Models Cannot Self-Correct Reasoning Yet** - first submitted 2023-10-03; inspected [v2, 2024-03-14](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.01798v2), §§3–4, 7; Tables 3, 7. GPT-4 GSM8K accuracy fell **95.5 → 91.5 → 89.0** after zero, one, two intrinsic-correction rounds; corresponding calls were **1, 3, 5**. On full GSM8K with GPT-3.5-turbo-0301, nine-response debate scored **83.0**, versus **88.2** for nine-response self-consistency. **Limit:** model/task/protocol-specific, not an impossibility theorem. Oracle stopping was a major confound in prior correction claims. Literal quotation checks establish provenance only; they are not a reliable oracle for entailment, relevance or scope.

3. **Du et al., Improving Factuality and Reasoning in Language Models through Multiagent Debate** - inspected [v1, 2023-05-23](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14325v1), §§2.2, 3.1–3.3, 5; Table 1. Three agents/two debate rounds achieved GSM8K **85.0 ± 3.5**, versus single-agent **77.0 ± 4.2** and majority **81.0 ± 3.9**. Their limitations explicitly report confident convergence on incorrect answers; §3.2 describes difficulty changing answers initially shared across model instances. **Limit:** extra compute and limited benchmark samples; Huang's larger, response-budget-matched comparison qualifies the general benefit claim. Neither paper implies same-model majority never works. Consensus is not source evidence, and distinct role names alone do not establish error decorrelation.

4. **Smit et al., Should we be going MAD? A Look at Multi-Agent Debate Strategies for LLMs** - first submitted 2023-11-29; inspected [v3, 2024-07-18](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.17371v3), §3 “Measuring the performance boost from debating,” “Improving MAD via agreement modulation,” and §4. Their Multi-Persona protocol reduced accuracy relative to its first agent's initial answer; authors attribute this to a devil agent designed to disagree even when the initial answer is correct. Agreement modulation helped, but the direction differed across datasets. **Limit:** predominantly GPT-3.5 multiple-choice QA; settings did not transfer well to Mixtral 8x7B. This argues against mandatory objections, not against all adversarial review. No agreement-percentage or prompt sweep is proposed here.

5. **Wang et al., Large Language Models are not Fair Evaluators** - first submitted 2023-05-29; inspected [v2, 2023-08-30](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.17926v2), §2.2, Table 2. Swapping Vicuna/ChatGPT response order changed GPT-4's comparison outcome on **37/80** questions and ChatGPT's on **66/80**. **Limit:** pairwise response-quality evaluation, not source NLI or a direct adversarial-review experiment. Transfer to parent/reviewer ordering is a risk hypothesis only; adding an anti-bias instruction was insufficient in their template.

## Two prospective variants after the fixed first hypothesis

These are untested architectural hypotheses, not completed-reject reruns or prompt/model/temperature/example searches.

- **Complementary evidence channels:** use two isolated, label-blind reviewers with non-overlapping work: quantity/polarity versus condition/attribution. Each returns at most one literal quote pair in the existing contract; neither sees the other's output. Hypothesis: task partitioning adds distinct evidence coverage without repeated NLI classification. Cost: two full-source reads; shared omissions remain possible. Do not count matching outputs as votes.
- **Evidence-triggered scope expansion:** keep the first independent reviewer; reserve call two for checking the surrounding original-source scope of its nominated span, rather than another opinion. Persist only a replacement quote pair exposing a governing condition, negation or speaker boundary within existing caps; if unavailable, abstain. Hypothesis: local scope repair reduces misleading-but-literal evidence. Cost: serial latency; a missed first nomination remains unrecoverable, and quote caps can hide necessary context.

For either variant, **`none` means no qualifying evidence returned, never agreement or proof of correctness**. Malformed/nonliteral evidence is separately invalid, not a semantic finding. Parent authority remains unchanged. Only quotes/structured checks are persisted, not hidden reasoning. Future authorized assessment must separate corrections from regressions and account for input/output tokens, calls and latency; none was run here. No code, inference, tests, grader, experiments, git, protected cases or banned files were accessed.
